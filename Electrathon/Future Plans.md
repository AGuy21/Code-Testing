Interactive 3D Car Model:

Use React Three Fiber to render a 3D model of your car directly on the site. Users could rotate it, zoom in, and click on parts (like the motor, battery, wheels) to learn about the engineering behind them.
Live Telemetry Dashboard:

Create a "Dashboard" page that visualizes data. Even if it's not real-time from the track, you could upload logs from previous races to show graphs of Battery Voltage, Amperage Draw, and Lap Times. It would look very high-tech and impressive to judges/sponsors.
"Build Log" or Blog:

A timeline or blog section where you document the construction process with photos and short updates. This is great for the "Engineering & Design" aspect of the competition documentation.
Sponsor Portal / Tiered Showcase:

Enhance the sponsor section to be more interactive. Maybe "Gold" sponsors get a dedicated profile card with a link to their site and a quote about why they support STEM.
Race Day Countdown & Weather:
* A widget on the home page counting down to the next race, perhaps integrating a weather API to show the forecast for the track location.
